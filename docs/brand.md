# Brand

## The idea

The mark is a maker's punch with ef struck through it; the e and the f are one bar. It is a hallmark: efforge records what it generated and will not overwrite what you changed.

For readers who cannot see it: a square stamp, with its top-left and bottom-right corners cut off at 45 degrees. Through it runs a lowercase e joined to a lowercase f. The e's bar runs on to the right and becomes the f's crossbar, and the bar's free end is cut on the same 45 degree bevel as the stamp. Every stroke has the same thickness. Next to the mark, the wordmark spells efforge in a semi-condensed sans.

efforge is not affiliated with Effect or the Effect team. Do not combine the mark with Effect's logo, and do not recolour it to look like Effect's.

## Change it

1. Edit `scripts/brand/source.ts`: the mark's paths, the palette, the wordmark or the repo's URLs. The lockup's canvas and the ligature's box are measured from the paths, so nothing else needs changing.
2. Run `pnpm brand`. It needs nothing but Node.
3. Commit. `test/brand.test.ts` fails while any generated file is stale.

Changing the words in `copy` (the social preview's sentence, the command, the badge label) also needs `pnpm brand:outline`, which outlines them with the fonts from the dev dependencies and writes `scripts/brand/text.ts`. It also checks that the vendored wordmark still matches the font.

## Using the mark

- Never round the cut corners, rotate, skew, or add a gradient, glow, shadow or outline.
- Do not put the one-colour mark on a mid-tone where it loses contrast. Use Iron on light, Paper on dark, or the colour mark.
- Clear space: one cell (1/16 of the mark's width) inside a tile, four cells on a page.
- The mark is drawn on a 16 x 16 grid, so it is sharpest at multiples of 16 px. Below 16 px, use the ef ligature alone, without the punch (`ligature-light.svg`, `ligature-dark.svg`).
- Orange is not part of the identity.

## Typeface

The wordmark is Saira Semi Condensed SemiBold (600) by Omnibus-Type, outlined to paths, so nothing needs the font installed. Its x-height equals the height of the mark's e. The social preview sets its sentence in Saira Semi Condensed Medium (500) and the command in JetBrains Mono Medium (500). Both typefaces are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, taken from `@fontsource/saira-semi-condensed` 5.3.0 and `@fontsource/jetbrains-mono` 5.3.0.

## Terminal

No banner. `create`, `add` and `list` start with one line: a blued-steel cell reading `ef`, then `efforge <version>`. It uses truecolor where the terminal says so (`COLORTERM`), the nearest 256-colour otherwise, and plain text under `NO_COLOR`, in a pipe and in CI. `--json` prints none of it.

## Palette

Straw, Bronze, Temper purple and Blued steel are the colours steel passes through as it is tempered, in that order. Assay red marks refusals and conflicts, nothing else.

Pairs the assets use: Paper on Blued steel 6.11:1 (the colour mark's letters, the avatar, the terminal cell); Iron on Paper 15.44:1 (the light lockup, the social preview); Paper on Iron 15.44:1 (the dark lockup, the badge text, the command on the social preview); Blued steel on Iron 2.52:1 (the badge's punch edge, a shape); the badge's edge, Steel at 35% over Iron, on GitHub dark `#0d1117` 2.77:1 (a shields.io `#555` label is 2.53:1); Blued steel on GitHub light `#ffffff` 6.66:1 and on GitHub dark `#0d1117` 2.83:1 (the punch's edge; on dark the letters inside, at 6.11:1, carry the mark).

Text needs 4.5:1 (WCAG AA); shapes and large text need 3:1. Where the "On Iron" ratio is below that, the next column gives the value to use on Iron and other dark backgrounds. Straw and Steel are too light for text on Paper: use them as fills behind Iron text (Iron on Straw 9.79:1). Bronze is 4.09:1 on Paper, so it is for shapes, not text. The tempering sequence for diagrams is Straw, Bronze, Temper purple, Blued steel, one colour per layer, always in that order.
